**Organization:** TPT Solutions (`tpt-solutions`)

**Crate Prefix:** `tpt-med-*`
**License Strategy:** Strict MIT-Only Chain

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Designing orthopedic implants, cardiovascular stents, and prosthetics requires intense, FDA-mandated Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and fluid dynamics. Legacy biomechanics tools (FEBio, OpenSim) often carry restrictive academic or copyleft licenses that prevent commercial SaaS wrapping. MedTech companies desperately need to run simulations in the cloud or in-browser to collaborate with surgeons, which legacy desktop Fortran/C++ solvers cannot do.

`tpt-medical` provides a cloud-native, browser-compilable (WASM) simulation stack for surgical planning, implant design, and FDA submission generation, strictly adhering to 21 CFR Part 11 audit requirements.

## 2. Substrate Leverage
*   **`tpt-fem`**: Non-linear hyperelastic material modeling, large-deformation contact mechanics.
*   **`tpt-science`**: Incompressible Navier-Stokes for bio-fluids (blood, synovial fluid).
*   **`tpt-engineering`**: Biocompatible material libraries (PEEK, Nitinol shape-memory, porous titanium, cortical/cancellous bone).

## 3. Crate Taxonomy & Data Models

### Core & Ingestion
*   **`tpt-med-core`**: Patient models, anatomical coordinate systems (e.g., ISB recommendations), HIPAA-safe audit traits.
*   **`tpt-med-dicom`**: DICOM to STL/Voxel meshing pipeline. Includes Hounsfield Unit (HU) to material density mapping for patient-specific bone models.

### Biomechanics & Implants
*   **`tpt-med-biomechanics`**: 
    *   *Data Models:* `HyperelasticMaterial`, `TissueLayer`.
    *   *Math:* Mooney-Rivlin, Ogden, and Yeoh strain energy density functions for soft tissue and elastomers.
*   **`tpt-med-implants`**: Bone-implant interface contact modeling, micromotion analysis, and fatigue life prediction for orthopedic screws and plates.
*   **`tpt-med-nitinol`**: Superelastic and shape-memory alloy constitutive models for stent deployment simulations.

### Hemodynamics
*   **`tpt-med-hemodynamics`**: Blood flow simulation, Wall Shear Stress (WSS) calculation, and Oscillatory Shear Index (OSI) for predicting aneurysm rupture or stent thrombosis.

### Compliance
*   **`tpt-med-fda`**: 21 CFR Part 11 compliant event sourcing. Generates immutable, cryptographically signed audit trails of every simulation parameter change for FDA 510(k) submissions.

## 4. Dependency Graph & MIT Chain
*   **Allowed:** `tpt-fem`, `tpt-science`, `tpt-math`, `dicom-rs` (MIT), `nifti` (MIT/Apache), `ndarray` (MIT/Apache).
*   **Banned:** `FEBio` (custom restrictive license), `ITK` (Apache-2.0 allowed but heavy, prefer lightweight Rust voxelization), `VTK` (BSD but massive C++ footprint, breaks WASM).
*   **Rendering Strategy:** Use WebGL/WebGPU via WASM for the 3D anatomical viewer to avoid heavy C++ rendering dependencies like VTK or OpenGL.

## 5. Verification & Validation (V&V) Strategy
*   **ASME V&V 40:** Follow the ASME V&V 40 standard for assessing credibility of computational modeling in medical devices.
*   **ISO 10993 / ASTM Standards:** Automate test cases based on ASTM F2028 (tibial trays) and ASTM F2079 (intramedullary rods).

## 6. Phased Delivery
1.  **Phase 1:** `tpt-med-dicom` (Patient-specific bone meshing and density mapping).
2.  **Phase 2:** `tpt-med-biomechanics` (Hyperelastic soft-tissue and bone FEM).
3.  **Phase 3:** `tpt-med-nitinol` & `tpt-med-hemodynamics` (Stent deployment and blood flow).
4.  **Phase 4:** `tpt-med-fda` (Immutable audit trail generation).

## 7. Business Wedge
**Web-Based Stent Deployment Simulator.** Sell a white-label web component to MedTech companies (e.g., Boston Scientific, Medtronic). Doctors can upload a patient's CT scan, and the WASM-compiled FEM solver simulates the stent expanding in the specific artery in real-time, directly in the browser.

## 1. Open Source Declaration

`tpt-medical` is a fully open-source, MIT-licensed computational biomechanics and medical device simulation engine. Every crate, every constitutive model, and every regulatory audit tool is freely available under MIT OR Apache-2.0.

### Why Fully Open Source for Medical?

The medical device and biomechanics simulation space suffers from three critical bottlenecks:

1.  **Proprietary Black Boxes:** Tools like Abaqus, Ansys, and FEBio (via commercial wrappers) charge exorbitant fees, hiding their solver mechanics behind NDAs.
2.  **Academic License Traps:** Open-source biomechanics tools (OpenSim, SimVascular, FEBio) often rely on restrictive academic licenses, heavy C++ dependencies (VTK, ITK), or GPL-adjacent meshing tools (Gmsh), making commercial SaaS integration legally and technically painful.
3.  **Cloud Privacy Risks:** Modern "cloud simulation" requires uploading patient DICOM scans to third-party servers, creating massive HIPAA/GDPR compliance hurdles and data breach risks.

`tpt-medical` eliminates all three:

*   **No License Barrier:** MedTech startups and enterprises can integrate it into proprietary surgical planning software without copyleft contamination.
*   **Zero-Cloud Patient Privacy:** Because it compiles to WebAssembly (WASM) and runs purely in Rust, patient DICOM scans can be processed **entirely in the browser or on a local hospital workstation**. No patient data ever touches a cloud server.
*   **Reproducible FDA Submissions:** Open-source solvers allow the FDA and regulatory bodies to inspect the exact mathematics used, increasing credibility under ASME V&V 40.

#### `tpt-med-viscoelastic`

Time-dependent tissue behavior.

```rust
pub struct ViscoelasticMaterial {
    pub elastic_model: TissueModel,
    pub prony_series: Vec<PronyTerm>,
}

pub struct PronyTerm {
    pub g_i: f64,      // Relative modulus
    pub tau_i: f64,    // Relaxation time (seconds)
}

impl ViscoelasticMaterial {
    pub fn relaxation_modulus(&self, time: f64) -> f64 {
        // G(t) = G_inf + Σ G_i * exp(-t / tau_i)
        let g_inf = 1.0 - self.prony_series.iter().map(|t| t.g_i).sum::<f64>();
        g_inf + self.prony_series.iter()
            .map(|t| t.g_i * (-time / t.tau_i).exp())
            .sum::<f64>()
    }
}
```

## 13. Summary

`tpt-medical` is a fully open-source, MIT-licensed, pure-Rust computational biomechanics and medical device simulation engine covering:

- **Imaging** — DICOM parsing, segmentation, voxel-to-hex meshing
- **Solid Biomechanics** — Hyperelastic tissue, bone remodeling, viscoelasticity
- **Hemodynamics** — Blood flow CFD, FFR, Windkessel boundary conditions
- **Implants & Devices** — Stent deployment, orthopedic micromotion, wear simulation
- **Surgical Planning** — Osteotomy, implant sizing, virtual surgery
- **Regulatory** — FDA 21 CFR Part 11 audit trails, ASME V&V 40 credibility

All crates maintain the strict MIT chain, leverage the TPT substrates (`tpt-math`, `tpt-engineering`, `tpt-science`, `tpt-fem`), and compile to WebAssembly for zero-cloud, privacy-preserving surgical planning.

No proprietary cores. No academic license traps. No cloud data exfiltration. Just open source.
